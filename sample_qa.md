## Known limits

1. **Statement/account procedures are out of scope.** The 5 Groww scheme pages
   don't describe download/statement/KYC/redemption processes, so those
   questions return *not found* (e.g. "How do I download my capital gains
   statement?"). Same for "how to invest/buy" process questions.
2. **Tax questions are not covered.** e.g. "What is the capital gains tax on
   ELSS?" → *not found* (no tax content in the corpus). The guardrail was fixed
   this phase so "capital gains" no longer triggers the performance refusal
   (see `tests/test_guardrails.py`).
3. **NFO-risk source quirk.** Sources list both `Riskometer (risk grade): Very
   High` and `NFO risk: Moderately High`. The assistant reports the risk grade;
   the NFO field is not surfaced.
4. **Cosmetic non-breaking hyphen.** The model sometimes writes "lock‑in" with a
   non-breaking hyphen (U+2011). Stored/displayed data is correct UTF-8.
5. **Freshness footer = ingestion date.** `Last updated from sources: 2026-10-12`
   reflects when ingestion ran, not live values; NAV carries the source's own
   "as of 09-Oct-2026" date.
6. **Direct-plan growth schemes only.** Regular plans and other AMCs are out of
   scope by design.
